# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY
Artificial Intelligence is a technology that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, recognising objects, analysing information, and making decisions.

Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, use AI, sensors, cameras, and advanced computing systems to move along roads and respond to traffic conditions. These vehicles can detect pedestrians, recognise traffic signals, identify road markings, and select suitable driving actions.

Examples of companies developing autonomous driving technology include Tesla, Waymo, and Mercedes-Benz. Their systems differ in their levels of automation and capabilities.

# WORKING PRINCIPLE

Self-driving cars operate through the following steps:

1. Data Collection: Cameras and other sensors collect information about the road, traffic, pedestrians, and surrounding objects.

2. Perception: AI algorithms analyse the collected data to recognise vehicles, road markings, traffic lights, pedestrians, and obstacles.

3. Localisation: The vehicle estimates its current position using GPS, maps, and sensor information.

4. Prediction: AI predicts the possible movements of pedestrians, cyclists, and other vehicles.

5. Path Planning: The system selects a safe and suitable route based on the destination, traffic conditions, and detected obstacles.

6. Decision-Making: The vehicle determines the appropriate action, such as braking, accelerating, stopping, or turning.

7. Vehicle Control: Electronic control systems operate the steering, brakes, and acceleration according to the planned action.

8. Continuous Monitoring: Sensors repeatedly collect new information, allowing the vehicle to update its decisions as road conditions change.


# RESULT

The study of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was completed successfully. The roles of Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Sensor Fusion, GPS, Path Planning, and Decision-Making in autonomous driving were understood.

It was learned that AI enables vehicles to identify road objects, predict traffic movements, plan suitable routes, and control vehicle operations based on sensor data. Although self-driving cars offer potential benefits in transportation, accessibility, and traffic efficiency, challenges such as adverse weather, technical failures, cybersecurity, and safety limitations remain.
